    Sources & method

    Terrain comes from USGS 3DEP elevation at 30 m. Forest includes deciduous, evergreen and mixed forest in the 2016 National Land Cover Database. The study boundary follows iNaturalist place 192775.

    North spans 315°–45° and south spans 135°–225°. Both require forest and at least 10° of slope. Trail proximity uses the historical NY State Parks network, merged in 2021. Verify current cover and access before planning a visit.
