Tutorial 08.3, Additional Representation in Webmapping
https://designpractices.org/tutorials/10-interactive-mapping-part-3/
The observation layer reads categorical month colors with get. Contour width
uses step on elevation (300 and 500 m breaks). Slope fill uses an aspect
category; symbols change with zoom. The page adds a linked detail extent,
slope and trail-proximity filters, updated whole-study totals, and linked
observation popups. Popups can list multiple nearby records.

Data
USGS 3DEP elevation, slope, aspect and shaded relief, 30 m analysis grid:
https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program
NLCD 2016 New York land cover:
https://cugir-data.s3.amazonaws.com/00/90/31/fgdc.html
Historical NY State Parks trails, 2021 merge:
https://services.arcgis.com/1xFZPtKn1wKC6POA/ArcGIS/rest/services/NY_State_Parks_Trails/FeatureServer/0
Community-defined study boundary:
https://www.inaturalist.org/places/192775
The Assignment 3 analysis identified north- and south-facing forested slopes
of at least 10 degrees. Areas were measured in the original 30 m UTM grid.
The polygons preserve cell boundaries. The near-trail filter uses the original
100 m proximity mask. Display figures are rounded to whole hectares.
Assignment 2 supplied 34 unique Kalmia latifolia observations from April–June
2025. Observer names, dates, accuracy and original links are in the GeoJSON.
Photos and unverified flowering-stage interpretations are omitted.

Interactive sampling revision
The landscape opens a slope comparison at the selected location. The example
button uses the original detail extent. Selecting a colored slope places a pin
on the nearest original 30 m candidate cell centre of the clicked aspect and
trail-proximity class. Elevation, slope and aspect come from the original grids.
Separation uses the great-circle distance between cell centres. Slope and
bearing are rounded to whole degrees. Returning restores the overview camera.
Codex implemented this revision in response to Nicole's interaction feedback.

Guided observation comparison
The trail-side example retains the Assignment 3 camera extent. A separate
observation sequence first locates M23 and M30, then overlays their sampled
slope classes before opening exploration of all 34 observations. Dates,
attribution and reported accuracy come from the original records; elevation,
slope and aspect are rounded samples of the Assignment 3 terrain grid.
The recorded positions are 224 m apart in UTM zone 18N. Their dates are
eight days apart; flowering-stage and microclimate effects are not inferred.
Nicole selected this sequence after reviewing the overlap analysis. Codex
implemented it and prepared the two-record export from the sampled GeoPackage.
