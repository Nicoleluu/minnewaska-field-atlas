Data & interpretation

Terrain uses a 30 m USGS 3DEP elevation grid. Forest includes the 2016 NLCD deciduous, evergreen and mixed classes. North spans 315°–45° and south spans 135°–225°. Both require slopes of at least 10°.

Distances are measured to the historical NY State Parks trail network (2021 merge). Current access and temperature require field verification. The 34 iNaturalist mountain-laurel observations are unevenly distributed across April, May and June 2025.
